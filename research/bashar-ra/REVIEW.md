# Review ledger

This study uses model-agent implementation and independent model-agent review. These are documented computational checks and source reading, not human peer review or an expert-consensus claim. Private receipts retain exact source/code/output hashes.

## Source and acquisition review

The independent source check reconciled all 106 Ra session IDs, 5,153 printed turns and 278,601 whitespace words against 536 source/extraction artifacts. It identified four missing-question placeholders, the orphan response at 103.3, bracketed editorial material and omitted footnote markers. The original audio inventory was checked against every saved player page. A separate header pass measured all 2,620 source lengths, preserving one initial 503 and its successful isolated retry.

The audio collector required tests and a real pilot before expansion. Independent review verified preserved channel counts, complete decoded duration within a declared 50 ms tolerance, source/output hashes, bounded storage reservations, failure receipts and cleanup only after a verified retained derivative. Bashar source validation required actual acquisition-proof hashes and rejection of unexpected video streams. The explicit resource-floor and temporary-PCM options were independently tested without changing historical defaults or records.

## Numerical and interpretive review

The baseline model review checked source IDs, eligible denominators, matrix dimensions/symmetry/diagonals, reference exclusions, topic weights and artifact hashes. Full neural-token coverage was independently reconstructed. Length sensitivity materially reduced lexical cosine values; source-aligned clustering remains confounded by editing, dates, speakers, genre and recognition.

A later review found that the original neural name masker could remove parts of contractions such as “don't” and “we'll”. Corrected run04 was independently accepted after all 30 tests, complete retokenization and 37 output hashes passed. It processes 610,730 document wordpieces in 2,611 chunks and 319,281 eligible question/response wordpieces with zero truncation. The correction changed some document cosines by as much as 0.0693, so earlier neural outputs are superseded. Lexical exact-token preprocessing and its results are unchanged. Earlier runs and inspection receipts remain preserved; no output is silently relabeled as corrected.

Source reading separately examined fixed passages, targeted counterexamples and retrieval leads. Failures include advertising mistaken for doctrine, magnetic versus ethical polarity, tarot-image vocabulary, printed but lost questions, and generic semantic matches across unrelated questions. The corrected top five retain the first four earlier leads but introduce a different fifth. The source reader inspected its actual Ra80.15–80.17 context and interview interval, distinguishing an archetypal spirit framework from people described as being in spirit. A new attestation binds the corrected ranking; earlier pilot readings remain historical. A bounded ASR reading is not full audio verification or human annotation.

## Interface and publication review

The first integrated browser checkpoint used the actual baseline and reading data. It passed 178 checks with no runtime/console errors, covering six views, three widths, source filtering, dialog focus, graph controls, CSV exports, missing-data recovery and synthetic-data rejection. It identified and fixed a dialog focus-cycling defect. This checkpoint does not validate subsequent data changes.

Final corrected numerical outputs, supplemental official-excerpt results, final collection receipts, manuscript, browser checks and Pages hashes must be reconciled before the release is described as complete. Final evidence and remaining acquisition limits will be recorded in the handoff; the present ledger records completed checks without pre-claiming the final release.
