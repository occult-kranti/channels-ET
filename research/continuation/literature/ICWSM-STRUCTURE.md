Current full-paper guidance: AAAI two-column format, initial maximum 11 pages including references (8 recommended), R&R/camera-ready 12 pages; mandatory checklist after references and before optional appendices; checklist and optional appendices excluded from the page count. Full-paper review is double blind. Dataset/demo routes have their own rules. Generative AI cannot be an author; content-generation use must be disclosed as required by the applicable track. A long living paper with TBD authorship/ethics/human validation is a research draft and is not thereby a compliant submission. No venue choice or submission is inferred.

The 2027 style manages Times-like fonts itself; instructions prohibit loading alternate text-font packages or altering the style, margins, spacing and figure geometry to squeeze the paper. Figures must have embedded fonts and no Type 3 fonts. A future submission needs its current exact instructions checked again. Do not modify the official style to fit the living draft.

## Composition lessons from actual sample papers

- **YouNiverse:** first establishes the platform problem and resource, then collection/preprocessing, ethics, dataset description and bias. Figure 1 separates source-specific collection from filtering; later distributions and bias plots have explicit units. Adapt this ordering, not its sample weights or scale.
- **Tube2Vec:** opens with a concrete example separating topic resemblance from social position. The validation-task schematic precedes performance tables. Use an analogous distinction between same topic, same question, answer resemblance and proposition agreement; show the actual evaluated task before its metric table.
- **Chen et al.:** separates narrative, visuals and comments into research questions, gives a common collection frame and branch-specific codebooks/models/validation. Our paper should similarly separate metadata, captions, digest Q&A, model outputs and independent judgments.
- **Ota et al.:** shows chronological upload-position trajectories, engagement distributions and a proxy transition network. In our corpus, upload position is not calendar time and a comment/source link is not a viewing path. Include only executed, supported plot families.

A useful living-paper sequence is: introduction with motivation and concrete unit ambiguity; contextual background on channeling as attributed discourse; related work organized by direct studies/platform collection/representations/model evaluation; explicit research questions; data and identity/provenance; executed methods and controls; source availability and failures; results with denominators and source pointers; model-assisted interpretations; disconfirming/negative results; limitations and alternative explanations; future falsifiable work; reproducibility, rights/ethics/authorship/AI disclosures; references and supporting appendices. Main-text tables and figures must do a job for a research question rather than expand a metric inventory.

For this release, favor acquisition-flow and evidence-layer diagrams; duration distributions; concentration/coverage plots; keyword denominators; matched representation perturbations; Q&A lookup examples; and measured model-arm comparisons. Plot absolute counts on zero-based scales and label any focused difference scale. Equal-account ranges and leave-one-account-out ranges are influence diagnostics, not population confidence intervals. No table of planned jobs may masquerade as newly collected material. Every figure needs actual data inputs, units, cohort, denominator and a nearby material limitation.
