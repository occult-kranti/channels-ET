## Decisions that remain bounded

No successful new raw caption or video collection is reported. Ordinary requests
returned a YouTube proxy CONNECT 403 and a separate author-source HTTP 403 despite
the desired unrestricted policy. The source queues remain prepared and incomplete;
no alternative route was used to evade the denials. Metadata-only records are not
transcripts, publishing accounts are not practitioners, and entity labels are not
verified speakers.

The 949 Q&A candidates remain one-context AI digest paraphrases. Their source
hashes support traceability rather than transcript accuracy. Proxy families reduce
observed overlap but cannot exclude unknown performance reuse or pretraining
contamination. Dependent-pair results do not carry independent-pair confidence
intervals. Eight held-out model questions cannot establish general superiority;
the 40-token model cap and uncapped lexical copying remain disclosed limitations.
Exact evidence substrings do not establish relevance, entailment or factual truth.

Human prompt-equivalence and answer-entailment labels, attributable answers from
multiple practitioners, complete channel histories, speaker validation, equal-
evidence model evaluation, rights review, institutional determination and human
authorship remain TBD. Hypotheses and falsifiers for these studies are specified
in the living manuscript. Hosted publication and actual conference submission are
separate from verified local builds and repository delivery.
