The independent AI critic accepts the bounded refresh implementation at
SHA-256 `624a71d1842a1eda897536b125d0ce5b7c2c732963cdff0c128b47034ce179d2`
for one execution after the coordinator commits the reviewed files and checks
out the matching clean `main`. This is a code and guard review, not a claim
that recognition, a new numerical fit, or a future deployment has completed.

Twenty-nine watcher tests passed independently. They exercise advanced remote
HEAD, dirty/index/untracked files, changes during copying or committing,
private fields and raw turns, closed publication structure, undeclared retry
sources, stale reviews, source partitions, dry-run behavior and no-push
behavior. Actual read-only checks replayed the pinned v4 policy against both
initial failed attempts, accepted both exact independently reviewed alternate
manifests, and returned pending while the actual queue was running.

The watcher admits only the declared 229-publication frame. Completed
standard recognition, exact linked captions and failed recognition holds must
partition that frame without overlap. Both declared alternate editions need
their own exact terminal-manifest and receipt verdicts. Additional automatic
holds remain visible and unadmitted under the pinned v4 policy; additional
alternate trials cannot silently enter. The merged edition retains the
standard candidate count separately from alternate receipt counts.

The critic required corrections to an impossible persistent-disk staging
budget, preservation of durable fit/provenance files when staging in RAM,
recognition of the producer's actual running status, closed public-field
validation, and the explicit two-alternate/v4-policy contract. Only eleven
metadata/figure destinations may change. Frozen earlier joint, written,
semantic and companion-paper studies remain outside that release allowlist.
Full source texts, model weights, recordings and private SQLite stay private.

A final chronology check found that refinement overwrote an already retained
raw epoch-placeholder date after the analyzer had nulled its interpreted
date. The refiner now preserves existing raw date and date-status fields.
Six collected-audio tests passed independently, including this regression.

`06bb8510102c74d91185936a7cf2b1ac34957ed366f6b2b4ea7f5f6c20652037`.

Publication requires the exact reviewed clean local and remote HEAD, repeats
checks before and during mutation, validates all changed blobs and the commit
parent, and uses a non-force push to the declared repository. Unexpected
files, changed sources, insufficient resources or incomplete/unreviewed
states stop. No failed race is repaired with a reset or destructive cleanup.
A race may leave the watcher's own enumerated local changes or an unpushed
commit for inspection. The finite watch has a deadline; it is not a promise
that all transcripts will be usable or that the environment will persist.

A future fit still requires its recorded output checks. This review does not
establish ASR accuracy, speaker identity, channelled-entity equivalence,
causal interpretation, statistical significance, or human peer review.
