## Bounded reading findings

**Mother Mary across two mediums.** Glasson's [Do You Trust the Universe?](https://omna.org/do-you-trust-the-universe-by-mother-mary/) frames distrust as disconnection from love and recommends reconnecting with a generous universe. Kribbe's [High Sensitivity](https://www.jeshua.net/channelings/mother-mary/high-sensitivity/) discusses emotional over-involvement, self-doubt and an inner-child exercise for boundaries and play. Both offer reassurance, but their practical framing differs. One selected message per medium does not establish stable doctrinal agreement or disagreement.

**Mary Magdalene across two mediums.** Glasson's [Grace Embrace Initiation](https://omna.org/grace-embrace-initiation-by-mary-magdalene/) uses initiation, grace and crystalline-consciousness imagery. Kribbe's [The Fire in Your Belly](https://www.jeshua.net/channelings/mary-magdalene/the-fire-in-your-belly/) emphasizes embodied feeling, suppressed anger/desire, female energy and grounded spirituality. Both describe inner transformation; lexical overlap must be interpreted alongside different dates, translations and publisher styles.

**A within-medium control.** Kribbe's [Isis message](https://www.jeshua.net/channelings/isis/the-spirit-of-freedom/) and Magdalene message share freedom, embodiment and female-energy themes, while her [Jeshua message](https://www.jeshua.net/channelings/mornings-with-jeshua/the-one-and-the-many/) emphasizes unity, diverse forms and non-hierarchy. A shared medium/translator/publisher can contribute to resemblance. Compare this control before attributing topics to a civilization or personality.

**The same label can conceal a different format.** Kenyon's [Hathor explanation](https://tomkenyon.com/who-are-the-hathors) emphasizes catalytic sound as the principal communication mode. Scranton's [January 2022 product description](https://danielscranton.com/downloads/january-2022-group-live-qa-with-the-hathors/) describes tones, a monologue and participant Q&A. Sound meditation, written exposition, interview and audience Q&A need separate genre fields before cross-medium topic and answer comparisons. Product descriptions cannot substitute for listening to the recordings.

These are attributed-text comparisons, not clinical advice, authenticated historical/religious statements, or tests of the claimed entities' reality. The register does not assign statistical significance or agreement probabilities. The methods agent fits the separately documented small text pilot and reports its limitations.

## Search and provenance method

The work applied the UAP evidence research skill's `codebook-provenance.md` and `review-gates.md`: document, claim, person and entity label are separate units; source access depth and missingness stay explicit; source claims are not external verification. Ordinary public publisher pages and conventional Brave/Yahoo search results supplied discovery paths. Query terms included channeling plus Michael/Gabriel/Raphael, Krishna, Shiva/Kali, Green Tara, Thoth, and exact Kristin Davies/Erik names. Publisher-native search supplied Arcturian/Pleiadian and Raphael pages. Search-engine AI summaries were ignored; discovery snippets did not become accepted direct-source claims.

Each attempted URL has a timestamp, status, title where available and hash/byte count when actual bytes were retained. A failed HTTP response, empty HTTP 200 response, search listing, category page, paid-product description and individual written message are different access/evidence scopes. Search rate-limit responses were retained, not repeatedly retried. No YouTube proxy bypass was attempted. No Reddit page was fetched directly or used as claim evidence; a search-results page incidentally containing a Reddit snippet was not treated as an authorized community corpus.

The source registry is reconstructible to its claimed scope through listed URLs and hashes. A future source may change or disappear; its present publisher output is not assumed identical to the captured version. Narrative claim confidence is about whether a source makes an attribution, not confidence that a spirit exists. Missing links between publishers are unresolved provenance, not evidence of independent origin.
