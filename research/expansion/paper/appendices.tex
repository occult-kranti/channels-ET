\section{Update Procedure and Acceptance}
Before expanding the corpus, preserve the source snapshots and define the unit, inclusion rule and access route. Execute a small pilot, retain successful and failed outcomes, then expand only when the pilot's success conditions are met. Record meaningful changes in code, dictionary, model, date interpretation, attribution or weighting. Recompute affected outputs and rewrite any dependent prose; old receipts certify only old artifacts.

For the present extension the acceptance checks are: (1) source pointers resolve to hashed inputs; (2) numerical summaries reconcile at their own grains; (3) unavailable comparison states do not render as zero findings; (4) dictionary and retrieval output can be reproduced; (5) queries and blocked requests are not counted as collected data; (6) figures and tables expose units and denominators; (7) the paper compiles from its package; and (8) the final review record names exact artifact hashes and unresolved limitations. Any unavailable check is reported explicitly.

\section{Practical Source Queries}
The database makes the following questions inspectable without claiming their substantive answer in advance: Which retained questions match a keyword family? Which candidates share wording across different uploads? Do their answer gists also share wording? Which retrievals mix interview or clip formats? Which questions lack a verified speaker or timestamp? Which source digest revision generated a row? A query that finds no candidate means no match in the released candidate database, not that a practitioner never discussed a subject.

\section{Collection Alternatives and Boundaries}
A supported API can replace public-page metadata collection when the appropriate capability and credential are available. Public author archives may supply licensed transcripts, and rights-cleared local audiovisual recordings may support speech recognition. Browser extensions can support a researcher's authorized local export when their data handling and license are reviewed. These are collection alternatives, not reasons to evade a denial. The present pipeline does not install opaque browser extensions, acquire private recordings or rotate identities to bypass a block. Any later integration must identify the exact software, version, source, permitted access and validation result.
