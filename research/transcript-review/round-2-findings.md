## Consequential findings and corrections

1. **A singleton is the selected audio partition.** The audio k=2 solution is exactly nineteen Bashar-related sources and the lone Athina source. Its cosine silhouette is 0.238345; stable seed labels describe this outlier split, not a demonstrated population or civilization distinction. The separate authored k=6 solution is weak (silhouette 0.057380) and contains singleton publications. The authors retained these results and added explicit sizes and limits.
2. **Negation preservation is incomplete.** The stoplist keeps negators, but audio `no`, `not` and `never` each occur in all 20 documents and are removed by max_df=0.95; `nor` is removed by min_df=2. Some bigrams still preserve negation. Fixed synthetic contrasts under the actual audio fit yield identical vectors for “Aliens made contact” versus “Aliens never made contact,” and for the corresponding “believe” versus “do not believe” pair. A third real/not-real pair remains distinct. These are counterexamples to stance interpretation, not an error-rate estimate or a reason to rewrite the declared primary fit.
3. **Strict zero does not establish absence.** Independent exact-string retrieval confirms strict Essassani/Sassani counts of zero, alongside four post-hoc `esasani`/`sasani` occurrences in four documents. Ashtar has three strict matches and fourteen additional proposed spelling strings in its single source. Segment/time/hash anchors are saved in `spelling-audit.json`. Variant identity and audio accuracy remain unvalidated; strict counts remain unchanged.
4. **Skeptical discourse shares vocabulary.** Purposeful independent reading of eight QA cases found support for their bounded paraphrases and candidate kinds. One source repeats belief/reality advice while criticizing it, providing an actual construct counterexample to interpreting lexical overlap as endorsement. The fragmentary 2027 passage preserves conditions; it is not an unconditional verified forecast. These eight cases are not a random sample or an annotation-accuracy score.
5. **Storage context and attribution coverage matter.** An early preview mislabeled Athina's feed catalog as Paul-context material. The final builder explicitly separates that catalog from episode speech and Paul. Publisher groups cover 886/942 indexed IDs, leaving 56 unassigned. Route, practitioner, entity and civilization associations have separate coverage and overlap counts. The final artifact exposes those denominators rather than treating all inherited group views as additive partitions.
6. **Graph sparsification differs from threshold diagnostics.** The displayed document graph is a top-two-neighbor union with cosine at least 0.20; threshold-sensitivity tables count all possible document pairs. These should remain visibly distinct in the paper and dashboard. The owner also replaced index-slicing self-neighbor assumptions with explicit self-index exclusion.

The 37 QA records are 21 audio question-like candidates of several kinds and 16 analyst-constructed authored comparison prompts. Their times and character ranges locate shared support windows, not verified question/answer turn boundaries. Source speech, authorship, original-session independence, semantic equivalence, medical efficacy and physical claims remain outside the validated scope.
