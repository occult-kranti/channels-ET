# Technical panel, round 1: design and disconfirmation

Status: method review completed before the new numerical and qualitative candidate freezes. Numerical, source-pointer and rendered-output findings will be recorded in rounds 2 and 3, against their actual candidate hashes. This panel is AI-assisted technical review, not peer review or human transcription validation.

The primary corpus is 20 completed ASR recordings: nineteen Bashar-related publisher episodes and one separate Athina/Ashtar creator episode. The second corpus is 16 authored publications: fifteen Daniel Scranton RSS items and one Paul Selig publisher PDF. These are source recordings/publications, not independent ET identities, practitioners, speakers, original sessions, or random population samples. The nineteen-to-one imbalance prevents defensible group-level estimates for an Ashtar population. Authored and recognized-speech results remain separate; any shared-vocabulary projection is explicitly confounded by modality, author and sampling.

The following design decisions and challenges were sent directly to the analysis, qualitative-reading and paper owners:

1. Fit primary lexical/topic models on the 20 ASR source documents. Expose the 16-publication authored analysis separately. Do not turn hundreds of transcript segments into independent practitioner observations.
2. Recompute counts and duration per unique source recording. Three ASR formats are three files, not three recordings or three times the audio duration. Publisher account/route groups and overlapping entity mentions must not become additive partitions without an explicit denominator.
3. Label lexical cosine, cluster assignment and topic weight as text measurements. Similar words do not establish agreement, shared origins, speaker identity, or physical ET claims. Questions, negations and criticism can create the same vocabulary.
4. Retain the chosen topic/cluster settings and all tested alternatives. NMF display k=3 is declared before results; candidate k=2–6, reconstruction errors and matched-topic seed stability remain inspectable. Exploratory silhouette-selected clustering is not an estimated count of natural ET groups.
5. Show network threshold sensitivity, source-ID nodes and edge definitions. Entity co-mentions are textual co-occurrence, not social, causal or physical relationships. Singleton/high-degree patterns can arise from long documents or host introductions.
6. Test length and source-format effects. Use document-level normalization, inspect raw/normalized term counts, and preserve a sensitivity to proper-name removal and the central 80% of ASR time. The latter is a positional trim, not verified advertisement removal.
7. Examine publisher concentration and leave-one-publisher-out topic matching. Multiple episodes from one show, repeated introductions, interviews and shared advertisements can drive apparent thematic structure. The lone Ashtar source has no within-group variance estimate.
8. Keep ASR names, errors, timestamps and missing/omitted speech uncertain. Source-file hashes establish byte lineage, not word accuracy. Whisper probabilities are not measured word-error rates. Host, guest, advertisement and attributed-entity speech remain unsegmented unless independently established.
9. Keep Daniel's full rendered RSS item words, including promotion/footer material, distinct from semantically separated messages. Paul Selig's publication is a spiritual-mediumship comparison, not a biological ET class.
10. Reading coverage must describe actual inspected source windows and words, not merely loaded or processed text. Exact ASR question/answer segment IDs, times, text hashes and source hashes must be recoverable even when the public candidate stores only paraphrases.
11. Retain uncertain, null, failed and contradictory exploratory outcomes. Conditional answers and question–answer linkage are interpretation candidates, with no human truth labels or verified speaker attribution. No inferred quotation should appear as exact speech.
12. Independent round 2 will read source bytes without importing author helpers, recalculate consequential lexical/numerical results and inspect adversarial source-pointer/construct cases. Round 3 will compare the frozen paper/dashboard values and visuals with the reviewed artifacts. Successful helper tests alone will not count as independent proof.

The provisional methods are suitable for a bounded, source-linked exploratory text study with these qualifications. Claims about populations, ET agreement/origins, diarized speakers or exhaustive channel coverage would require revision. The next two rounds remain pending actual candidate outputs.
