# Consolidate and audit

Use this workflow for the Atlas source-chunk/card retrieval experiment. Read the
actual literature register and experiment receipt before describing a method as
Google's sleep/distillation approach. Source-card consolidation alone is not
weight training or a reproduction of a training paper.

1. Freeze source inputs, card edition, query groups and relevance judgments before
   retrieval comparisons. Keep evaluation-query wording outside card generation
   and all indices. Record any author exposure and source-informed selection.
2. Compare raw source chunks, qualified cards and any declared hybrid using the
   same eligible source frame. Index card answer text and qualifiers, not the
   analyst questions used as navigation labels.
3. Retain source IDs, exact evidence links, conditions, dates and modality in
   consolidated records. Treat missing qualifiers as information loss, not an
   improvement in concise presentation.
4. Log retrieval rank, coverage, duplicates and abstention separately from answer
   correctness. A source hit is not a verified answer, truth or agreement.
5. If a qualifier-stripped or unrelated-query control is used, label it synthetic
   and keep it outside the empirical corpus and final knowledge cards.
6. Test proposed cross-field connections as hypotheses, with an observable metric
   and plausible alternative explanations. Do not promote a metaphor into a
   biological, spiritual or physical mechanism.
7. Publish actual execution settings, denominators, failed cases and uncertainty.
   Small AI-audited query sets support diagnostics, not broad retrieval accuracy.

Never overwrite the original evidence, present a rewritten summary as a quote,
or claim model weights changed because an index or document collection changed.
