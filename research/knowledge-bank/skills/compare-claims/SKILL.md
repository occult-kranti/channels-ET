# Compare scoped claims

Use the existing relation vocabulary in `CODEBOOK.md`; do not invent an agreement
probability from cosine scores or an entity-authentication score.

1. Identify the analyst question or actual documentary question and both source
   editions. If the questions differ, record that before comparing responses.
2. State each proposition's referent, conditions, time, modality, suggested action
   and source attribution. Check qualifiers in the original passage, not only a
   summary or embedding neighbor.
3. Classify a narrow shared proposition, compatibility, different focus,
   conditional tension, explicit contradiction or unresolved comparison.
4. Require a common proposition, referent, conditions and time before an explicit
   contradiction. Different council names, hypothetical stages or events often
   make that assessment unresolved. Optional exercises do not contradict the
   rejection of obligatory intermediaries or universal formulas.
5. Preserve a counter-reading and a qualification. Record negative findings,
   including zero confirmed contradictions, without extrapolating to the corpus.
6. Recheck every comparison after ASR or source-edition changes. Never transfer
   old character offsets or imply unchanged wording without validation.

Use embeddings for retrieval and exploration; use evidence to assess claims.
Repeated readings by AI roles are not human ground truth or independent reports
of the claimed phenomena. Do not endorse source health/financial advice or treat
predictions as successful without an explicit outcome evaluation.
