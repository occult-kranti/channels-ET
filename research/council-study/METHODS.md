# Council discourse study

This is a separate exploratory study of inspected publisher messages and recording contexts. It does not change the accepted Bashar–Ra, 292-unit joint, written-message, semantic, or publisher-audio studies. The publication and recording are the observation units; a council name is an attribution label rather than an authenticated organization or speaker.

## Source selection and editions

The research distinguishes the exact user wording **Council of Contract**, the independently supported **Council of Contact**, and source-specific Galactic Council labels. An Arcturian message discussing a proposed galactic council is a comparison control, not a Galactic Council speech. Publisher descriptions and discovery results do not enter the text model as if they were complete channeling messages. The source registers document the finite query/source frame, inspected depth, retrieval failures, aliases and unresolved leads.

The November 8, 2026 release fits five selected text editions, totaling **26,451 whitespace words**, from three medium contexts. Three Council of Contact recording transcripts contribute 9,033 words; one newly acquired Pamela Aaralyn interview contributes 16,931; one Daniel Scranton written Arcturian comparison control contributes 487. The four retained audios include one new download and three reused recordings. These are neither five verified entity speeches nor five independent channels. There are no cross-medium pairs sharing the same exact council label in this text frame.

Private manifests bind every selected text, raw source and retained-media receipt with bytes and SHA-256. The analysis freezes the exact manifests into a fresh private run, rechecks their artifact hashes and declared word counts, and copies the executed analysis code. Existing Contact recordings are classified as reused only after their acquisition receipts exactly match the pinned, previously completed 229-publication frame. The presence of an ASR receipt alone does not mean reuse. Newly acquired audio needs an explicit new-study origin and a receipt in this study's private archive; unresolved origin remains explicit.

Exact normalized text aliases are collapsed. A declared publisher-written/audio publication family contributes one preferred written-message edition to the main fit; the related ASR remains retained and listed as an alternate edition. The rule does not rank recognition accuracy. Unresolved underlying session, repost and excerpt relationships are not treated as proven independence. Full text and private SQLite remain outside Git and Pages.

The source frame has no non-Bashar minimum duration. Bashar retains a 300-second minimum. Lifetime YouTube-upload totals are unknown; successful independent publisher downloads are not counted as YouTube downloads without edition verification.

The reused Tracy audio uses full-duration 48 kbps Opus derivatives and the prior English-only Whisper tiny.en recognition editions. The admitted Pamela pilot uses a full-duration **22 kbps mono** Opus derivative and the already cached multilingual Whisper tiny model with previous-text conditioning disabled and one CPU inference thread. These are different audio/recognition representations, not a controlled accuracy comparison or an improvement claim. Compression/downmixing, language-model configuration, interview format and source context can all affect vocabulary and recognition. The document metadata exposes the retained codec/channel/average-bitrate and checkpoint hash/language mode where the receipts provide them. All machine-text utterance roles remain unknown; a program titled around a council does not make every host/guest utterance council speech.

## Vocabulary and sensitivity analyses

NFKC normalization and Unicode word tokenization preserve apostrophes and negation. The default mask removes **complete exact council-label and medium-name phrases**, longest first. It does not globally delete substantive words such as contact, light, council or divine. The exact phrase list is published with the analysis.

TF-IDF uses unigrams/bigrams, sublinear term frequency, smoothed IDF, L2 normalization, min_df1, max12,000 features, and the English stop list retaining no/not/nor/never. At least two texts with 30 masked analysis tokens are needed. A separately refitted unmasked representation measures sensitivity to label/name wording. Centered 300-token spans are selected before masking and transformed with the fixed whole-document masked vocabulary and IDF; short texts are excluded rather than padded. Interstitial digits and punctuation remain until phrase masking, so a title containing a number can still be removed correctly from a span.

All unordered document pairs remain inspectable. Comparisons distinguish same-medium/same-label, same-medium/different-label, different-medium/same-exact-label and different-medium/different-label. Literal shared labels do not verify shared entity identity, common origin, equivalent answers or independent testimony. Pair means are descriptive: the pairs are dependent, and length, publisher, medium, text format and attribution can be strongly confounded. No p-values or entity-origin probabilities are estimated.

With at least five eligible texts, at most three NMF factors and three KMeans clusters are displayed. This is an explicit display choice, not an optimized or validated doctrine count. KMeans seeds11/23/47 expose seed sensitivity; cosine silhouette and adjusted Rand similarity with medium labels describe structure and source confounding. A two-component SVD is a visualization coordinate system, not a map of beliefs. Below the threshold the dashboard reports insufficient documents instead of fabricating a topic or cluster fit.

In the five-edition release, NMF converges after 15 iterations. All three KMeans seeds reproduce a partition that **exactly coincides with medium context** (adjusted Rand index 1.0), while its cosine silhouette is only 0.02069. Seed agreement here does not establish robust doctrine categories: one short written control, one long mixed interview and three related Contact recordings confound topic, source, medium, date and recognition method. The three same-medium/same-label pairs average 0.14837 masked cosine; the seven different-medium/different-label pairs average 0.07818. Those dependent pair means cannot isolate council identity from medium or source format.

The declared keyword dictionaries count exact word forms in **unmasked** text per1,000 analysis tokens. They cover contact/disclosure, choice/agency, emotion/self-care, collective change, practices/attention and contracts/incarnation. Polysemy, negation, quotations and incidental mentions remain unresolved. These are lexical measurements, not doctrine prevalence or recommendations endorsed by this project.

## Source reading and question boundaries

The Contact reading layer uses AI review of the three complete retained machine transcripts. Every bounded paraphrase binds a source ID, text-artifact hash and unknown-role segment/time locators. The matrix compares how contact is framed, how the council is described, practices recommended by the source and the specificity of its timing language. These are contextual emphases rather than mutually exclusive categories or numerical credibility scores.

The constructed questions in this reading are analyst questions, not verified audience questions. Punctuation does not identify speakers, questions or answers. No answer is assigned from adjacent ASR text, and no same-question equivalence follows from lexical similarity. Audio word accuracy, speaker identity and temporal alignment have not been verified by listening. Relative-year statements remain reported claims; publication metadata is not converted into validated predicted-event dates.

Supplemental readings distinguish the complete Daniel written comparison control from selected windows of the Pamela interview. Each reading declares its selection rule, actual words and disjoint character spans read. Bounded paraphrases bind exact NFKC-normalized source substrings by SHA-256, word count and character locations; optional ASR turn/time endpoints are checked against the retained transcript. Full-text coverage is asserted only for a single span covering the complete retained body. Source attributions remain claims and no automatic speaker identities are assigned. These readings add contextual interpretation without adding model observations or claiming a complete doctrinal reading of a mixed interview.

The released Pamela annotation reads 6,364 of its 16,931 retained ASR words (512 of 1,376 segments) in six declared windows. It reports the interview's explicit transition from council questions into future-human material, alongside contingent timing claims and source recommendations. These are contextual paraphrases of claims, not verified forecasts, advice endorsed by the project, or authenticated speaker attributions. Independent review uses its own bounded frame and records that coverage separately.

## Separate existing-corpus retrieval

The accepted 292-unit joint corpus is reloaded through its existing hash-bound loader. Six declared exact phrases are searched in its retained texts, with case-insensitive whitespace/hyphen handling. Counts, source links and prior input hashes are public; matching context windows are private. Nested phrases can overlap, so their occurrence counts must not be added as independent claims. A phrase hit is a retrieval lead, not a source-local attribution, and zero hits do not prove absence outside this fixed corpus.

The executed retrieval finds two Council of Contact phrase hits: one in an Alcyone-titled recording and one in a Lyran Golden Dragons recording. Bounded AI reading of the two retained windows (261 words in total, not two complete documents) identifies a cross-reference to an intermediary council and an invitation for future topics, respectively. Neither hit establishes that the council is speaking in that passage. Public paraphrases bind exact source-text and context hashes, character locations and unknown speaker-role status; the original windows remain private. Independent review recounts the full 292-document phrase search and checks these two interpretations.

The example private manifest paths must refer to retained authorized source artifacts under the private workspace root. The output path must be fresh. `output-manifest.json` lists the exact public allowlist: `analysis.json` and bound SVG/PNG/PDF figures. Full text, raw source pages, SQLite, retrieval contexts and private path-bearing provenance are not in that allowlist. The stdlib-only release verifier checks denominator arithmetic, source joins, matrix symmetry/bounds, pair means, keyword rates, reading bindings and figure hashes. Independent source reading and numerical refits are recorded separately; passing structural checks alone does not establish scientific validity or transcription accuracy.

The actual final release adds the completed Pamela annotation to the already reviewed numerical fit using `enrich_readings.py`. It revalidates the frozen source manifests and every selected document, preserves all document/numerical/keyword/figure fields, and records the parent analysis hash plus a metadata-only enrichment receipt. This does not run a second numerical model. Independent refitting reproduces all TF-IDF cells within 4.44×10⁻¹³, all keyword counts/rates exactly, NMF weights within 2.08×10⁻¹⁴ and all three KMeans partitions. The tests cover source tampering, duplicate editions, masking, pair categories, reading coverage and metadata integrity; rendered source keys and fixed similarity scales were inspected separately.
