The fitted frame has **five text editions, 26,451 whitespace words, three medium
contexts and three source-specific council labels**. The four associated audio
files total **10,739.57725 decoded seconds and 42,871,099 bytes**. Only Pamela's
19,521,485-byte file is a new audio acquisition; the Contact files already belong
to the completed 229-publication collection. There is no new YouTube download or
lifetime-channel census claim. Full-duration Opus derivatives are lossy: Pamela
uses 22 kbps mono and the older Contact recordings use 48 kbps encoding.

The public explorer provides source/claim relationships, filters and inspectors,
actual acquisition counts, TF-IDF similarities with name-masking and length
sensitivities, keyword rates, three exploratory NMF factors, cluster diagnostics,
source-reading tables, 12 downloadable figure files and CSV exports. The separate
292-document retrieval study finds two incidental Contact references; these do
not become new Council speeches. No cross-medium body pair shares the same exact
council label in the five-text model frame.

The clusters match the three medium contexts (adjusted Rand index 1.0), with weak
cosine silhouette 0.02069. Format, length, recognition method and medium are
confounded; this is not evidence of distinct entities, shared origins or doctrines.
Contact reading covers all 9,033 ASR words; Daniel reading covers the complete
487-word message; Pamela reading covers 6,364 of 16,931 words in six specified
windows. Readings are source-linked AI paraphrases. Recognition accuracy, speakers
and forecasts have not been independently verified by listening or outcome study.

`collect_audio.py` and `finalize_audio.py` retain complete-source/derivative
provenance and resource admissions; `build_acquisition.py` projects actual
receipts. `analyze.py` fits a fresh private edition, `enrich_readings.py` attaches
checked reading metadata without changing the numerical results, and
`private_archive.py` builds a fresh private text bundle. Preserve accepted inputs
and use new edition paths for future collection/refits. Do not change the older
hash-pinned `research/batch-media/audio.py`.

Further body-level comparisons require additional accessible recordings or full
written messages, ideally multiple mediums with the same exact source-supported
label and comparable format/length. Metadata leads alone do not fill that gap.
The initial pilot deliberately retained one new full interview within the
remaining storage budget while the earlier collection completed. No unfinished
background downloader is implied by the presence of collection code.
