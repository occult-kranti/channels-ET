## Finite search and retention protocol

The search frame was deliberately bounded: exact/near-exact council-name searches, primary checks for the visible medium leads, and one complete publisher RSS response from a verified Apple podcast lookup. Queries included `channeling "Galactic Council"`, `"Galactic Council" "mp3"`, named-medium queries, and a quoted WordPress query on Daniel Scranton's publisher site. The latter returned one exact-phrase post in the requested response. The register records submitted URLs, HTTP status, retrieval timing when available, response hashes and observed outcomes.

Ordinary HTTPS used the configured environment connection. Brave results supplied discovery leads; these were followed to publisher pages where available. Search ranking is not a complete sample. Query operators may be relaxed by a search engine; lack of hits does not establish that a name or channel does not exist. Bing responses were irrelevant to the requested council in these trials. Google and DuckDuckGo responses included script/challenge pages and supplied no usable primary evidence. No challenge, login, payment or blocked host was bypassed.

Direct YouTube collection was unavailable in this environment. Retained search titles and publisher YouTube embeds therefore remain discoverable leads, not downloaded or analyzed videos. Examples include Shawna's `ZQmVzJIpf7Q`, Jamye's `-f9FX0gmIIQ`, Roger's `GMmPyzPaqbs`, and the Soul Journey Magics items `InGDzXBrYBI`, `OJumvrkTNyQ`, and `-q-B1o36_nM`. Non-Bashar sources have **no minimum-duration filter**. This pilot stops at the selected primary enclosure and reviewed source register; it does not claim that all channel uploads have been acquired.

Successful primary responses and search receipts were retained privately. Some failed requests were observed only in tool results; the ledger marks those with day-level timing and does not invent exact timestamps or response hashes. Important observed failures include Bonnie's official website (406), Rachel's page (406), the Airacle podcast page (403), its unverified candidate feed route (CONNECT 403), and a discovered Shopify page (404).

## Reproducible text and storage handling

[extract_galactic_sources.py](extract_galactic_sources.py) extracts Daniel's bounded publisher message from its opening speaker attribution through its closing signature. Navigation, advertisements and recommendations are excluded. The normalized UTF-8 message is **487 words**, with raw-source and normalized-text hashes in a private manifest. Its `corpus_kind` is `council_discussion_comparison_control`; its speaker remains **The 9D Arcturian Council**. Its publication instant is supported by article metadata, with the equivalent Hawaii-local schema timestamp retained as a date check.

The new 16,917,184-byte RSS response was losslessly gzip-retained at 2,336,665 bytes after byte-identical decompression verification. Four newly fetched metadata pages were similarly compressed, saving a further 3,863,751 bytes. Three additional new discovery pages were then losslessly compressed, saving 489,937 bytes; their derived discovery references and public hash-scope annotations were updated before removing the plain copies. Receipts distinguish compressed artifact hashes from decoded response hashes. Current bindings were updated before removing only those new uncompressed copies; prior media, accepted studies and the bounded message body were preserved. This is reversible storage compression, not content removal or sample filtering.

Full source bodies and recordings stay in the private research workspace. The public register contains metadata, paraphrases and provenance hashes. Workspace retention is not permanent ChatGPT storage or a Google Drive upload.

## Comparisons supported and still missing

The present evidence supports an exact-name/source-role graph: medium or publisher → claimed council label, with edge types for primary descriptions, mixed-recording attribution, message self-attribution and search-only discovery. It also supports a clear demonstration of why keyword matching alone overcounts council messages: Daniel's document contains the target phrase while attributing its voice to a different council.

Potential comparisons include authority and council membership, human participation, assistance during change, individual responsibility, and predictions about social events. These are **candidate coding questions**, not established cross-medium themes or findings from uncollected messages. Compare actual messages only after body access and speaker boundaries are established. Use publication-level denominators, distinguish introductions from attributed speech, preserve exact council names, and show missingness rather than filling gaps with biographies.

There is not yet a complete matched same-question, same-entity cross-medium dataset. Shared wording, shared names and thematic similarity cannot establish a shared speaker, independent corroboration, predictive accuracy or causation. Small-sample TF–IDF and nearest-neighbor results should remain descriptive; fitting more topic clusters does not create more independent messages.
