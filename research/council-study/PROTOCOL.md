# Council-name follow-on: exploratory protocol

This additive study follows the 8 November 2026 request for Galactic Council and
“Council of Contract” material. It preserves the earlier studies, their papers,
and the finite 229-publication publisher collection.

## Questions and units

We investigate which inspected sources actually attribute a communication to a
named council; what those communications describe as contact; and how vocabulary,
recommendations and timing language compare across the available source contexts.
The units are publication, retained media edition, text edition, and source-local
claimed name. A title, an incidental mention and an attributed message are
different kinds of evidence.

“Council of Contract” remains the user's literal search phrase. “Council of
Contact” is a separately evidenced name, not an automatically corrected alias.
Likewise, Galactic Council, Galactic Council of Nine, Arcturian Council, Galactic
Federation and other labels are not silently combined into one identity. Shared
words do not establish shared origin or independent corroboration.

## Collection and access

The discovery frame comprises explicitly logged ordinary web queries, inspected
publisher pages/feeds, and the existing private corpus. Search results are leads;
body-level findings require inspecting the source. Failed or irrelevant results
remain in the retrieval ledger. The English-language sample is purposive and
bounded, not an exhaustive channel census or a representative sample of religions.

Existing files are reused by exact source and artifact hashes, without claiming a
new download or counting another independent recording. New freely accessible
publisher media, if found, form a separate selected pilot. The initial new-audio
budget is 20 MB of retained full-duration Opus derivatives, with a separate 2 MB
recognition-output budget, to preserve capacity for the earlier running batch.
The recognition budget was tightened from an initial 8 MB to 2 MB before the new
pilot executed; complete output must fit or remain unadmitted. The old study
retains its separate 64 MiB final-fit reserve and existing disk floor.
No minimum duration applies to this non-Bashar study. Undownloaded discoveries
remain visible as metadata leads; a storage budget is not an estimate of the
publisher's complete archive.

Ordinary YouTube access has a recorded pre-origin CONNECT failure in the current
environment. Independently published audio is not counted as a YouTube download
without edition verification. Public Git and Pages receive metadata, code,
paraphrased findings and derived measurements; full media/text stay in the private
workspace. No permanent ChatGPT-account or Drive storage is claimed.

## Analysis and review

The methods record specifies actual preprocessing, exact-name masking, length
controls, TF-IDF, and any topic or cluster fit supported by the observed sample.
Small or confounded samples must remain explicitly exploratory. Retrieval from
the earlier 292-unit corpus is reported separately from newly attributed sources.
Unknown speaker roles remain unknown; punctuation and similarity cannot identify
answers, agreement or an entity's identity.

Source reading adds bounded paraphrases about contact definitions, council naming,
recommended practices and timing specificity, with source/segment locators.
These are AI-assisted interpretations of the inspected edition, not human
annotations, verified predictions or advice endorsed by the study.

Two source-research roles, computational methods, interface design and independent
criticism provide the five-role AI review. Their work and targeted corrections are
recorded in the review document. This is an exploratory protocol written during
collection, not a preregistered confirmatory study or human peer review.
