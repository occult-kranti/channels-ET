## 5. Copyright / re-upload landscape (notable)

- Bashar Communications has a published copyright policy and actively enforces it on YouTube. A fan "response video" (0OYF3W0UbVM, uploader Thomas Richardson / @intrestedparties) pushes back on takedowns and cites a 9-year-old re-upload with 513k+ views that had been removed. Several full-session re-uploads discovered in this strand were unreachable at digest time (HTTP 400 from the transcript service), consistent with removals.
- The bulk of Bashar content on YouTube is therefore **third-party clip/compilation channels**, not official. Uploaders observed in this strand (from transcript pages): Divine Light Vibes, Path of Knowledge, Free Vibration / Free Vibrations, 巴夏听闻录 (Chinese fan channel), ETs and Erin, Mammoth Awakening, Cosmic Bridge, QUANTUM HEALING PATH, Love Is The Way, Cosmic Soul, Extreme Mysteries (re-hosting Reuben Langdon's "Interview with Extra Dimensionals"). Title-pattern families seen in search: "Bashar Twin Flame -- …", "Bashar (Darryl Anka) 2025 | …", "… | Bashar Channeling 2026", "Bashar Reveals …", "[Full] Bashar :: … #1080p". Also a Portuguese channel "Essassani Bashar - Conhecimento Universal" (UChagKg0uHtbqkDR0pu5vmlQ).
- Several search hits were YouTube Music "podcast" entries (music.youtube.com/podcast/<id>) — these are audio re-uploads mirrored as podcasts, not official.
