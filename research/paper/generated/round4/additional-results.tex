\paragraph{Publisher-omission sensitivity.}
The matched title--caption comparison contains \AtlasNMatchedEligibleVideos{} eligible videos, of which \AtlasNMatchedContributingVideos{} contribute to the channel-level comparison because their \AtlasNMatchedPublishers{} publishers each have at least two records. The resulting \AtlasNMatchedPairs{} off-diagonal pairs give Spearman $\rho=\AtlasRhoMatched$. Removing one publisher and all of its incident pairs leaves ten publishers and 45 pairs. Across the eleven deterministic omissions, the correlation ranges from \AtlasLopoMin{} (omitting Pamela Aaralyn Channeling) to \AtlasLopoMax{} (omitting the official Bashar account). This range describes sensitivity to the included publishers; it is neither a confidence interval nor evidence of a statistically established positive or negative association. The pair values share publishers and videos and are not independent observations.

\paragraph{Caption-length heterogeneity.}
The \AtlasNCaptionPilot{} English-pilot caption documents contain \AtlasCaptionWordsTotal{} word tokens in total. Lengths range from \AtlasCaptionWordsMin{} to \AtlasCaptionWordsMax{}, with median \AtlasCaptionWordsMedian{} and linear first and third quartiles of 579 and 2,546.25 tokens. The shortest document is from hgrfrequencies and the longest from Lorie Ladd. This nearly 99-fold range motivates chunking within videos and equal weighting across videos when constructing publisher centroids. It does not establish the relative amount of channeling speech: captions also contain hosts, audience questions and promotional material, and their word counts reflect the specified tokenizer and available caption track.
