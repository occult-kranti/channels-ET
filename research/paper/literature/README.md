# Literature evidence for the working paper

Checked 23 October 2026. The 28 scholarly references in `../references.bib` cover direct channeling content studies, limited ethnographic context, religious and small-community platform research, account embeddings, and text-measurement validity. Selection was targeted, not exhaustive or preregistered. The manuscript should cite only the references needed for its claims.

- `source-register.json` and `.csv`: titles, authors, venues, dates, primary links, publication class, access depth, supported use, and limits for every BibTeX key.
- `annotated-bibliography.md`: readable original annotations. A downloaded full paper is not automatically recorded as deeply read.
- `related-work-synthesis.md`: the argument supported by the sources and implications for methods, figures and writing.
- `related-additions.tex`: four concise paragraphs prepared for manuscript round5.
- `round5-paragraph-proposals.tex`: optional expanded alternatives, not additional mandatory text.
- `round4-claims-review.md` and `round4-reviewed-inputs.json`: actual round4 review findings and hashes of the frozen inputs.
- `retrieved-fulltext-manifest.json`: hashes of temporary research inputs. The article bodies are not redistributed.

The official publisher or conference record controls bibliographic metadata. Author-hosted full texts are identified when publisher access failed. “Peer-reviewed” describes the publication category; it is not a claim that we inspected confidential reviewer reports or that the work validates an extraterrestrial origin. ICWSM poster papers are specifically labeled. MMTEB cites the official ICLR2025 version, not a later arXiv revision.

The source register supersedes Phase1 access notes where the manuscript reviewers subsequently obtained a full text. In particular, the 2023 quantitative correspondence subset is seven US participants, while its qualitative analysis includes fifteen people. Earlier frozen analysis-round artifacts remain historical records.

Useful open repositories inspected in the literature workflow include [Tube2Vec](https://github.com/epfl-dlab/youtube-embeddings), [Sentence Transformers](https://github.com/UKPLab/sentence-transformers), and the software links in `../../analysis/literature/bibliography.json`. Reading a repository does not mean its pipeline was run. Actual model and software execution is documented by the analysis manifests.

Primary-source searches combined the direct study titles/DOIs with channeling, mediumship, religious YouTube, small-community spirituality, channel embeddings, topic interpretation, reliability, and corpus documentation. Official ACL, AAAI, ICLR/NeurIPS, journal, author-institution, and supplement records were preferred. Secondary search results were discovery leads only. No model leaderboard or review article was treated as an empirical test of this corpus.
