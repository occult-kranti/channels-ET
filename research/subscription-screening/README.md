## Collection and analysis accounting

The [static audit dashboard](../../dist/subscription-audit/) separates indexed video IDs, unknown total uploads, downloaded media, retained full text, new analysis and historical analyses. `build_audit.py` joins exact case-insensitive handle routes to unique IDs in the preserved inventory; inherited handle associations are not a fresh uploader-identity verification. Missing current totals stay unknown, rather than turning a small sample into an entire channel archive.

Athina Król and The Galactic Guide each have two caption records in the historical numerical study. Its external raw-caption archive was not located in the current source roots. Those prior results remain visible, but their vectors and metadata cannot reconstruct full transcript text. Current publisher audio, YouTube editions, captions, ASR and authored articles have separate provenance and counts. A related podcast is not counted as a downloaded YouTube video without an exact edition match.

Collectors must enumerate finite supported publisher frames, preserve actual failures and source exclusions, verify complete media before transcription, and keep source/edition hashes. HLS downloads must abort when a fragment is unavailable; a nominally successful downloader exit alone is insufficient. New collections share the documented cloud storage reserve with the ongoing Ra/Bashar archive. Current ordinary YouTube access has a recorded pre-origin CONNECT denial; unchanged blocked routes are not retried across all accounts. Independently published official audio and text can still be inventoried and acquired through their ordinary public URLs.

The existing Bashar–Ra study, source archives and papers remain intact. New transcript analyses require actual retained full text and their own source-frame declarations. Historical title profiles or caption derivatives are never presented as newly acquired transcripts.
