# Separate publisher-written text recovery

The first OmNa audio collection correctly kept 149 pages pending when their visible message/author block template differed from the already inspected template. This supplement preserves that original observation and attempts a separate written edition from each exact retained page. It does not change audio receipts or turn publisher prose into an ASR transcript.

Two held pages were fully read by an AI reviewer: *10th Dimension Boost by Andromedans* and *Your Timeline of Ascension by Archangel Metatron*. Their complete message and closing occupy a unique Elementor `342945a` / `theme-post-content.default` wrapper. Navigation, comments and footer lie outside. The initial `Download Audio MP3` paragraph is a button, and the publisher author is explicitly in `article:author` metadata rather than a visible author paragraph.

`publisher_text_recovery.py` checks exact source bytes/hash, unique wrapper/type, unique Natalie Glasson author metadata, a closed HTML boundary, absence of navigation/comments/form/script/style structures inside the wrapper, the exact initial button paragraph, and a broad 50–5000-word structural bound. It removes only an explicitly inspected initial button/heading variant. For the 17 pages with a visible publisher attribution/rights notice, an exact unique notice marker bounds the message ending; the notice and optional `Find out more` link are retained in a separate private companion artifact. Independent review of all 17 endings found at least three mismatches: *How to Seek Liberation* closes as Raphael but links to Celestial White Beings; *Mother Earth's Secret* closes as Celestial White Beings but links to Pleiadians; *Your Relationship with Fear* closes as Raphael but links to Mother Mary. These preserved marketing footers must not determine a message's claimed speaker. The actual message ending remains in place. Every accepted body gets its own hash, UTF-8 artifact, HTML character boundary/hash, and selected-text character interval. A mismatch remains pending. The word bound is a template guard, not a content/duration policy for collection.

The first execution recovered 91 of 149 pages, totaling 91,151 words; 58 stayed pending because their initial paragraph differed. A subsequent source inspection found two exact variants: 41 older pages start with `Download Audio`, and 17 start with `Channeled Message` followed by `Download Audio MP3`. Three complete messages from the latter group and one complete message from the former were read before extending the rule. Two remaining notice-only boundary variants were inspected without claiming full-message reading. The final fourth edition recovers all 149 messages (150,251 words), with six full AI readings and 143 automatic extractions. All original 91 body hashes remain unchanged. Earlier outputs and review receipts are preserved. No human validation, current YouTube access, authenticated channelled identity, listening, or waveform equivalence is claimed. This source prose may contain unverified spiritual, health or energy assertions.

Public `publisher-text-recovery.json` contains metadata only. Private bodies and a private manifest remain separate. Join the supplement to an audio record only on exact `record_id`, `page_url`, and `page_sha256`, preserving edition histories. Ten regression tests cover wrapper duplication, author mismatch, embedded navigation, prefix mismatch, alternate exact prefixes, optional marketing footers, private-path sanitization, inline/paragraph normalization, and retention of the closing.

python research/youtube-archive/verify_text_recovery.py NEW_EDITION
python -m unittest discover -s research/youtube-archive -p test_publisher_text_recovery.py
