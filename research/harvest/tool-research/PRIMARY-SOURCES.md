# Independently published source material

The original 48-row podcast index is retained at `research/deep/repo_raw/data/podcasts.json`; its bytes match the original Bashar repository input. This audit does not change that index or infer new YouTube identities. The inventory workstream separately preserves its original row edges.

The source chain used here is **original indexed Apple collection/episode link → Apple catalog's publisher feed URL → publisher RSS identity and episode GUID/title/date → that RSS item's enclosure URL**. Fourteen distinct podcast collection feeds were retrieved. The returned feed windows are not assumed complete histories. Apple is a directory bridge; publisher/feed metadata do not establish legal ownership or the truth of what an interview says.

The fixed acquisition frame is `research/harvest/publisher_sources/expanded-plan.json`: nineteen independently identified original publisher podcast editions from the twenty-two Apple-linked index rows. Two full audio files were initially saved and independently checked before expansion. Remaining downloads, validation failures, hashes and measured durations appear in `publisher_sources/publisher-summary.json`, rather than in this discovery document.
