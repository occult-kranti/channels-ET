# Bashar (channeled by Darryl Anka) — Source-Cited Reference

Compiled 2026-09-11 from public web sources. Every factual claim carries a source URL. Where sources disagree or a claim could not be verified, this is flagged explicitly. Quotations are kept short (≤30 words). Nothing here endorses the truth of channeled claims; the reference describes what Bashar Communications, Darryl Anka, secondary summaries, journalists, academics and skeptics say.

**Source quality key used throughout:** `[official]` = bashar.org / tv.bashar.org / store.bashar.org / Bashar Communications materials; `[primary-interview]` = Anka speaking in his own words; `[academic]`; `[press]`; `[skeptic]`; `[secondary]` = fan wikis, blogs, summaries (treat with caution); `[commercial-listing]` = bookseller/streaming metadata.

## 1. Who / what

### 1.1 Bashar, as described by Bashar Communications

- Bashar Communications describes Bashar as "a physical E.T, a friend from the future who has spoken since 1984" through channel Darryl Anka, and says the material "is based on the laws of physics and is not just a nice New-Age philosophy." `[official]` https://www.bashar.org/about
- Conscious Life Expo's (Bashar-supplied) listing calls Bashar "an extraterrestrial being from the future, a 'contact specialist' who is specifically involved in preparing Earth for extraterrestrial contact." `[official/event]` https://expo.consciouslifeexpo.com/bashar-channeled-by-darryl-anka-1
- Mayim Bialik's Breakdown (March 24, 2026) introduces Bashar as "a multidimensional, physical extraterrestrial hybrid being from a parallel reality" and a "First Contact Specialist." `[press]` https://www.bialikbreakdown.com/episodes/aliens-are-making-contact-3000-years-ahead-warning-were-making-big-mistakes-darryl-anka-bashar
- In the official "A Message from Bashar," Bashar says the purpose is "to reflect to you certain things" and to "share certain perspectives," never to elevate "any belief system … as being more valid than any other," summarised as "all truths are true and the truth is made of all truths." `[official]` https://www.bashar.org/message
- BasharTV's introduction page says the teachings have been shared "Over the past 40 years" and lists "The 4 Laws of Creation" among core modules — note the four-law phrasing there versus five laws on bashar.org (see §3.1). `[official]` https://tv.bashar.org/pages/introduction
- Bashar Communications, Inc. states it "was formed by Darryl Anka and April Rochelle," that Anka began channeling in 1983, and that its objective is "to be the catalyst for the continuous generation of new, transformative information by Bashar." `[official]` https://www.bashar.org/bashar-comm
- April Rochelle is identified as CEO of Bashar Communications; the company had by 2014 "published Bashar's information in over 20 books" and distributed "hundreds of CDs and DVDs." `[primary-interview, BATGAP ep. 236, June 14, 2014]` https://www.batgap.com/darryl-anka-bashar/
- Rochelle also reads audience questions at live events (event format: monologue → "Ask Bashar" Q&A chosen by raffle → closing meditation/exercise). `[official]` https://www.bashar.org/event-faqs
- Disclaimer: all products/services are "for educational purposes only"; the company makes "no guarantees of accuracy of information" and disclaims medical/legal advice. `[official]` https://www.bashar.org/disclaimer

## 2. Essassani / Sassani — what Bashar says about his world

- **Name and location.** Essassani is glossed as "place of living light," said to be about 500 light-years away toward Orion. `[secondary]` https://en.everybodywiki.com/Darryl_Anka ; Anka: "about 500 light years in the direction of the Orion Constellation," in an alternate reality invisible to us. `[primary-interview]` https://batgap.com/darryl-anka-bashar-transcript/ Gaia's series blurb: "500 light years away, Bashar, a humanoid being from the parallel reality of Essassani." `[commercial-listing]` https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/84234/messages-from-bashar-the-extraterrestrial
- **Sassani = the people; Essassani = the world.** Fan summaries use "Sassani" for the inhabitants and describe them as the third of five hybrid races. `[secondary]` https://hybridchildren.com/hybrids-explained/what-is-the-sixth-hybrid-race/ ; https://cosmic-contact.de/en/blogs/extraterrestrisch-ufos/sassani-das-frohliche-sternenvolk
- **Hybrid origin.** Baroni summarises: the Sassani are hybrids engineered by the Zeta Reticuli ("humans from a parallel dimension") from human and Grey DNA to restore Zeta reproductive capacity; this frames abduction narratives. `[academic]` https://www.cesnur.org/2012/el-baroni.htm The early session "The Legacy" (c. 1988, published 1990) has Bashar state his race are "the children of the Greys / Zeta Reticulum and humans," contrasting Zeta mass-consciousness with human individuality. `[secondary session summary]` https://ethealing.nl/en/our-media/library-of-human-et/bashar-event-overview/civilizations-open-contact/the-legacy
- **Five hybrid races → sixth.** Bashar's "The Five Hybrid Races" (April 28, 2018, Anaheim) describes each race's name and function, with the Sassani "the third race of five" and humanity "becoming the sixth hybrid race." `[secondary summary of official listing]` https://www.ethealing.nl/en/library-for-human-et/17-the-five-hybrid-races Fan summaries give the ladder as: 1st (a "complete failure"), 2nd (ET-looking, neurological issues), 3rd Sassani/Essassani, 4th (unnamed, increasingly human), 5th Yah'yel/Shalinaya, "those who will come first," and 6th = evolved humans with enhanced empathic/psychic capacities. `[secondary]` https://hybridchildren.com/hybrids-explained/what-is-the-sixth-hybrid-race/ **Note:** the sequence "Yahyel→Shalanaya→Sassani" in the task brief is not supported; sources treat Shalanaya/Shalinaya as *another name for* the Yahyel (theyahyel.com: "The YahYel are also known as the Shalanaya"). `[secondary]` https://theyahyel.com/2016/09/15/bashar-interview-future-earth-hybrids-essassani-channelling/ In a 2009 session Bashar calls the Shalinaya "the first ET civilization to have open contact with earth." https://ethealing.nl/en/our-media/library-of-human-et/bashar-event-overview/civilizations-open-contact/the-span-2010-2015

## 3. Core frameworks

### 3.1 The Laws of Creation — five-law and four-law versions (both official)

**Five-law version (bashar.org/fivelaws, verbatim):** `[official]` https://www.bashar.org/fivelaws
1. "You exist"
2. "Everything is here and now"
3. "The One is the All and the All are the One"
4. "What you put out is what you get back"
5. "Everything changes except the first 4 Laws"

**Four-law version** (older/parallel phrasing; used by BasharTV's intro "The 4 Laws of Creation" and in Baroni's paper "Bashar's four laws"): `[official]` https://tv.bashar.org/pages/introduction ; `[academic]` https://www.cesnur.org/2012/el-baroni.htm ; text as given in a secondary bio: 1 You exist; 2 The One is All and the All are One; 3 What you put out is what you get back; 4 Change is the only constant (except the first three laws). `[secondary]` https://en.everybodywiki.com/Darryl_Anka
The difference is the insertion of "Everything is here and now" as Law 2 in the five-law version. The Iasos summary (1990s–2000s) already lists five with "Everything is Here/Now" second. `[secondary]` https://iasos.com/metaphys/bashar/

### 3.2 The Follow Your Excitement Formula (five steps, official, verbatim) `[official]` https://www.bashar.org/formula
1. "Act on your excitement, your passion, whatever is most exciting to you, in the moment. Do this every moment that you can."
2. "Do this to the best of your ability. Take it as far as you can go until you cannot take it any further."
3. "Act on your excitement/passion with absolutely no insistence, assumption or expectation of what the outcome should be."
4. "Choose to remain in a positive state regardless of what happens."
5. "Constantly investigate your belief systems. Release & replace the un-preferred beliefs: fear-based beliefs, and the beliefs not in alignment with who you prefer to be."
Iasos calls this "the Essassani instruction manual" and says following it activates synchronicity, "the organizing principle of the entire universe." `[secondary]` https://iasos.com/metaphys/bashar/ Anka's 2024 book *The Formula* expands it (see §5).

### 3.3 The Seven Sequential Steps of Manifestation (official; from an August 1995 session) `[official]` https://www.bashar.org/sevensteps
1. Vision — "Visualize what you want."
2. Desire — "Be intensely excited about what you are visualizing."
3. Belief — "Believe what you desire is possible to manifest."
4. Acceptance — "Accept your belief and your ability to manifest it as being true."
5. Intent — "Want and intend are different. You must have the intention to manifest your desire."
6. Action — "Act and behave like your desire has already manifested."
7. Allowance — "Detach from the outcome…"

### 3.4 Bashar's Basic Principles (13, official, verbatim) `[official]` https://www.bashar.org/principles
1. "You are a non-physical consciousness that is experiencing physical reality."
2. "You are here on Earth at this time because you chose to be. You were created in the image of the Creator, your essential essence is unconditional love, and the experience of ecstasy is your birthright."
3. "The highest purpose of your life is to be yourself to the best of your ability and live each moment as fully as possible."
4. "You always have free will and the freedom to choose."
5. "Anything you can imagine that is relevant for your life's theme is possible for you to experience."
6. "You attract your life experiences through the interaction of your strongest beliefs, emotions and actions."
7. "Excitement is the physical translation of the vibrational resonance that is your true, core natural being. Follow your excitement!"
8. "You are naturally abundant, and your choices are always supported by creation."
9. "There is actually only one moment in creation. Everything you experience is the same moment from a different point of view."
10. "You create the past and the future from the here and now."
11. "You are an eternal being and while you may change your form, your consciousness cannot cease to exist."
12. "Everything you experience is another aspect of yourself."
13. "You are loved so unconditionally by Creation that you can even choose to believe that you are not loved."

### 3.5 Physical mind vs. Higher mind
- Iasos's summary: the Higher Self/Higher Mind "operates outside time" and knows how a desire will manifest; the physical mind "cannot know how"; the recommended division of labour is "Physical mind designs WHAT you want; Higher Self knows HOW it manifests," with imagination "the pipeline through which your Higher Self talks to you." `[secondary]` https://iasos.com/metaphys/bashar/
- The official session "Crossing the Threshold and Tipping the Scales" (2012) is described as exploring "higher-mind reflection and physical mind's polarized responses." https://ethealing.nl/en/our-media/library-of-human-et/bashar-event-overview/transformation-new-earth?start=10
- A 2022 summary lists "overcoming fear through the physical mind" and "higher self" among Bashar's recurring themes. `[secondary]` https://sheilaprycebrooks.substack.com/p/bashar-and-his-channelled-teachings

### 3.6 Beliefs → emotions → thoughts → actions
- Principle 6: experiences are attracted "through the interaction of your strongest beliefs, emotions and actions." https://www.bashar.org/principles
- Quote attributed to Bashar: "All emotions stem from beliefs. You can't feel something without believing something to be true first." https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/tag/bashar
- Iasos: "Believing is Seeing"; beliefs set the frequency you vibrate at; the process runs mental (blueprint) → emotional (activation) → physical (action); to change a feeling, identify the belief, "own" it, replace it. `[secondary]` https://iasos.com/metaphys/bashar/
- The "three-step 'what would I have to believe' process" as named in the brief could not be found under that exact label in any fetched source; the closest sourced formulation is Iasos's three-step belief-change method above and Formula step 5. **Unverified label.**

### 3.8 Parallel realities / "shifting"
