## 1. "Oh No, Ross and Carrie!" (Maximum Fun) — Ross Blocher & Carrie Poppy

Investigative-comedy podcast in which the hosts personally attend fringe events and report back. They covered Bashar three times, all at the Conscious Life Expo (LAX Hilton, Los Angeles).

Show tags reference Darryl Anka, channeling, the vortex, Law of Attraction, and expo MC Jimmy Church. ### Ep. 377 — "Ross and Carrie Transmit Bashar (Part 1): Very Telling Edition"
- Date: 2023-06-25
- Episode page: https://maximumfun.org/episodes/oh-no-ross-and-carrie/ross-and-carrie-transmit-bashar-part-1-very-telling-edition/
- Transcript: https://maximumfun.org/transcripts/oh-no-ross-and-carrie/transcript-oh-no-ross-and-carrie-ep-377-ross-and-carrie-transmit-bashar-part-1-very-telling-edition/
- Summary (paraphrased from transcript): The hosts returned to the Conscious Life Expo for a premium Bashar workshop (roughly $45–55 a ticket, ~800 people, reportedly the expo's best-attended session). They describe Darryl Anka (then 71, a former film visual-effects artist and Paul Anka's nephew) visibly entering trance with jaw-clacking and head jerks before the greeting, then speaking rapidly as Bashar with quips they read as condescension wrapped in humor, plus constant disclaimers that he offers "probabilities," not predictions. They note the "formula" (follow your highest excitement; green-light/red-light synchronicity) is recycled as the answer to nearly every question. Critiques: vague language that leaves escape hatches; refusal to engage politically sensitive questions; blame routed back to the questioner's beliefs; and a contact "window" from 2023 to 2033 made conditional on human behavior, hence unfalsifiable. They highlight a 2010 documentary clip in which Bashar concedes the material could be Darryl's imagination — the "very telling" of the title. Show tags: Willa Hillicrissing, Blue Light Technique, permission slip.

### Ep. 378 — "Ross and Carrie Transmit Bashar (Part 2): Follow Your Excitement Edition"
- Date: 2023-07-10 (page updated 2023-07-28)
- Episode page: https://maximumfun.org/episodes/oh-no-ross-and-carrie/ross-and-carrie-transmit-bashar-part-2-follow-your-excitement-edition/
- Transcript: https://maximumfun.org/transcripts/oh-no-ross-and-carrie/transcript-oh-no-ross-and-carrie-ross-and-carrie-transmit-bashar-part-2-follow-your-excitement-edition/ (PDF: https://maximumfun.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/ONRAC-Ep.-378-Bashar-Part-2.pdf)
- Summary (paraphrased from transcript): Continues the same workshop through the audience Q&A (questions about humor, parenting, synchronicity, life direction). The hosts found the performance entertaining but note Bashar's rapid-fire jokes and habit of shutting down follow-ups with "already answered that." The audience is described as devoted and validation-seeking. Their analysis: Bashar's "universal principles" often mirror Darryl Anka's own biography; the red-light/green-light synchronicity idea is too vague to test; the claim that different followers inhabit different "versions" of reality insulates the teaching from contradiction; Bashar avoids firm predictions and declines medical advice while still floating wellness suggestions such as "detoxing." Overall verdict: a charismatic self-help act whose authority rests entirely on the alien persona rather than on any demonstrable evidence.

Note: Both hosts left the show in 2024 (farewell episode transcript exists on maximumfun.org); no further Bashar episodes were found.

- Dualistic Unity (Medium), "The Illusion of Cosmic Wisdom: How Bashar Exploits Spiritual Seekers," 2024-09-20 — https://medium.com/@dualisticunity/the-illusion-of-cosmic-wisdom-how-bashar-exploits-spiritual-seekers-133c52d511bb. Argues that claimed extraterrestrial authority places Bashar beyond accountability and that slogans like "follow your highest excitement" are ambiguous enough to create interpretive dependency. Also contends the material exploits emotional vulnerability with promises of manifestation while dodging responsibility when predictions fail.

- James Fletcher (Medium / Lumen Obscura), "Darryl Anka: The Man Who Says He's Channeling Aliens — And Why People Are Listening," 2025-04-22 — https://medium.com/lumen-obscura/darryl-anka-the-man-who-says-hes-channeling-aliens-and-why-people-are-listening-65dd233ca30c. A descriptive profile with subtle editorial distance (Anka as "a bald, tight-eyed man speaking in rapid, robotic tones") that explains the appeal without endorsing or debunking. Useful as neutral context on audience size and cultural positioning rather than as a critique.

- Mayim Bialik's Breakdown (Substack), "Interview with the Alien?", 2026-03-27 — https://bialikbreakdown.substack.com/p/interview-with-the-alien. Post-interview reflection in which Bialik voices "left-brain" skepticism ("what do you mean this alien hybrid is talking through you?") and says she wants scientists such as Neil deGrasse Tyson to weigh in. Tone is curious rather than convinced, while acknowledging followers report benefit.

- IBTimes UK, "'Alien Messenger' Bashar Says Humans Are Making 'Big Mistakes' Ahead of Predicted Contact by 2027," Crisnel Longino, 2026-03-25 — https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/alien-messenger-bashar-says-humans-are-making-big-mistakes-ahead-predicted-contact-2027-1788142. Included as evidence of how the 2027 prediction is entering wider press.
