# Independent critical review: channel and recording laboratory

This record is an independent AI review within the implementation team, not human peer review, conference acceptance, verification of channeling claims, or human transcription validation. The reviewer owns this document and separate audit harnesses; the source frame, analysis, recognition pipeline, readings and interface have different owners. Earlier knowledge-bank reviews and accepted corpora remain separate.

## Milestone 1 — design critique

The useful primary question is **how stable are cross-source text comparisons when duplicate editions, document length, attribution names, recording format and unequal source representation are accounted for?** A source-bound descriptive answer is attainable. A census of YouTube channels, a test of shared entity identity, or an estimate of doctrine prevalence is not supported by the retained purposive collection.

The following objections were sent to the relevant owners before the new outputs were reviewed:

| Threat | Required response and decisive check |
| --- | --- |
| Repeated publications and alternate ASR editions inflate the sample | Distinguish publication, underlying recording, text edition, publisher account, human medium and attributed speaker. Freeze one canonical analytic unit, retain an alias ledger and verify source/hash joins. Exact identity checks do not prove all remaining documents independent. |
| Group means conceal uneven source counts and compositional weights | Show document/word/format counts and missingness; distinguish equal-document means from a mean over all words or pairs. Normalized TF-IDF weight is not a proportion of statements or topic prevalence. |
| Shared observations invalidate independent-pair inference | Treat pair counts as descriptive. Compare within-group neighbor frequency with its availability baseline and macro-average groups when reporting a balanced summary. No pairwise significance claim. |
| Length controls accidentally change the candidate population | Full-text and bounded-window neighbors must use the same candidate universe; disclose any short-document exclusions and exact window lengths. |
| Written prose, interviews, captions and machine transcripts differ systematically | Retain origin, language basis, machine model and unknown-speaker status. Separate written/ASR strata and describe mixed-language or uncertain sources. New editions require rereading before inheriting old annotations. |
| Titles, proper names and later information leak into claims | Fit body representations; disclose name masks. Publication dates are not proof of prediction time or temporal validation. Any genuine holdout must predate fitting and selection. |
| A single topic seed becomes a purported doctrine | Fix the topic count, compare multiple seeds with explicit alignment, report assignment and term stability, and keep exploratory partitions separate. Do not name machine clusters as verified teachings. |
| A shared printed label is mistaken for authenticated identity | Keep observed labels, medium attribution and analyst entity hypotheses distinct. Similar wording or a shared name does not establish the same speaker. |
| Two-dimensional proximity or network density substitutes for source evidence | Display projection distortion and original-space similarities. Thresholded edges and visual layouts are exploratory; expose edge rules and sensitivity. |
| Chunk timestamps are presented as exact word timing | Require complete sample coverage and canonical waveform identity. Window boundaries are not word alignments, speaker labels or transcript accuracy measurements. |
| Selected qualitative examples exaggerate novelty or contradiction | Freeze purposeful case rules before new rankings; retain failed candidates. Bind complete exact passages and conditions, distinguish documentary from analyst questions, and require shared proposition, referent and time scope for a contradiction. |

The minimum informative analysis is a verified canonical inventory, document-level original-space neighbors, group composition, a small set of prespecified length/name/format/balancing sensitivities, seed stability, and a bounded set of source-linked readings. Additional plots must answer one of those questions rather than multiply unsupported claims.

The reading owner froze twelve purposeful candidate cases before inspecting new rankings. The quantitative owner committed to fixed eight-topic NMF with seeds 11, 23 and 47 and explicit topic matching; separate exploratory eight-group K-means; original-space TF-IDF and MiniLM; bounded-window and name-mask controls; and retained numerical witnesses for independent arithmetic. These commitments are plans until the executed outputs and their bindings pass review.

All selected text artifact hashes were checked; exact body normalization, words, row order, selected-edition joins and declared group counts agreed. These checks validate source binding, executed measurements and arithmetic. They do not validate the truth of source claims, word accuracy or the semantic correctness of all 493 texts.

## Milestone 3 — correction and publication verification

No unresolved scientific or provenance blocker remains for this bounded descriptive release. The review identified three additional interface defects before acceptance: equal-width progression bars misrepresented a short final text chunk and omitted actual fractions; object-valued recognition flags were ignored; and zero-valued diagnostic results and singular limitation fields could disappear. The design owner corrected these and included actual-data browser assertions. The final design-owned browser receipt records 29 core groups and three separately identified fault simulations, with no response interception in the core checks. The independent reviewer inspected the decisive root source/edition cross-bindings and exact-file-set publication checks. Root reports 51 focused tests and the prior-study/static-publication gates passed. Those browser and root test results are owner evidence, not a claim that this reviewer repeated each test.

The accepted scientific modules are `registry.json` (SHA-256 beginning `21124666`), `analysis.json` (`59f08ddd`), `collection-coverage.json` (`5d987fea`), `readings.json` (`46a25899`), `batch-asr.json` (`3d5e8c11`) and `asr-effects.json` (`f24e59af`). Final documentation/manifest refresh and deployment are integration steps; they may not silently replace these reviewed scientific artifacts.

Two observed results deserve prominence over reassuring averages. The publisher-macro full-versus-bounded neural neighbor Jaccard is only 0.050657: this particular bounded content is a poor substitute for the complete texts, without isolating length as a causal variable. The seed-47 NMF mean matched-factor cosine is 0.897876, but its weakest factor match is 0.287818; not every factor is stable. The four-source Mary/Magdalene aggregate favors the within-context comparison in all four representations, with individual-pair exceptions. It does not establish universal style dominance, doctrine separation or common speaker identity.

## Limits that a conference-style review would retain

The frame is highly imbalanced: Tracy Kumbera and Ra account for 309 of 493 units, and 11 of the 26 publishing contexts contain only one unit. Equal-context averaging changes that weighting; it does not create missing within-context replication or remove purposive selection. Fourteen creator IDs denote attribution contexts, not fourteen independently identified people: official and guest Darryl Anka contexts are separate IDs, and some other IDs describe hosts or session contexts with unresolved voices.

The current contribution is an auditable collection-and-measurement workflow with inspectable sensitivity, not a validated general theory of channeling discourse. The qualitative cases are purposeful AI readings without an independent human annotation sample. The lightweight pretrained encoder is a reproducible baseline, not a newly trained or state-of-the-art model. Topic stability, neighbor enrichment and shared vocabulary cannot identify an external entity, establish influence or determine truth. A publication claiming those stronger outcomes would fail this review even if every software and arithmetic check passed.
